## What it is

- **Playable now.** A single static HTML card game hosted on GitHub Pages — no app store, no sign-up, no backend.

## The business case

- **Dwell time becomes rounds.** A quick card duel between rounds keeps groups at the table longer.
- **Loser buys the round.** Built-in social pressure to order the next drink, not a discount.
- **Screenshot UGC.** The game generates roast-the-loser images; players share them, carrying your name with them.
- **Optional app-install funnel.** If Wetherspoon ever wants to drive the app, the game ends with a soft “check the app for this week’s prices” step.
- **No social-media dependency.** Works inside WhatsApp groups and pub tables; it does not need feed algorithms or paid reach.
